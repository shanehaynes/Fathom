Design prototype · 04

A spectrum under a dark ocean

The screen is the surface and you are looking straight down into the water. One prismatic sheen, hues always slowly shifting, drifts about the plane on its own. Depth is the z toward you: asleep far below at night, rising toward you during the wake, fully visible a few feet down by day. Where it drifts is always chance. The interface speaks only in warm white — the water owns every other color.

Committed to a single dark theme by design. The sheen is generative — six slow radial blooms whose hues drift independently around the full spectrum. Motion on the x/y plane is constant and undirected; the depth parameter moves only the fictitious z, driving brightness, saturation, and apparent size, and it changes only when the time-of-day state changes. No water is simulated; the feeling is carried by depth, slowness, and light. Selected controls speak in warm white only, so the spectrum is the sole source of color in the app.
